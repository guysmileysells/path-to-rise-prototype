## Owner contract
Add five tools, preserving original five content, order, timing, ratings, audio and program isolation. Exact additional names: Physical Tension Reset; Control and Confidence Reset; Re-Engagement Reset; Urge and Craving Reset; Self-Compassion and Accountability Reset. All tools retain What / When / How / Why / Benefits / Duration / Adaptations / Scientific References in that order; 3–5 When/Benefits examples, numbered How, no clinical guarantees. Audiences include first responders, military personnel and veterans.

- PMR: gentle 5-second tension / 10-second release, hands/shoulders/legs/feet followed by relaxed jaw; 2- and 5-minute options; skip painful/injured areas, no forced breath, forceful tension or jaw clenching. Exact adapted timing is a prototype sequence, not a validated protocol. Two minutes = 10s prepare + four 15s muscle slots + 10s relaxed jaw + 40s natural rest. Five minutes = 20s prepare + three rounds of those four slots + 20s relaxed jaw + 80s natural rest. Skipped areas become natural-rest slots, retaining the selected total. Skips remain memory-only.
- Control: concern (no required free text), control/influence/outside control, constructive action, if–then plan and first-responder examples; 1–3 minutes.
- Re-engagement: acknowledge state, select movement/nature/social connection/personal care/enjoyment/accomplishment, choose smallest safe meaningful action, begin when appropriate; 2–5 minutes to initiate. Selection remains memory-only.
- Urge: recognize, optional intensity 0–10, neutral physical observation, notice change without promising reduction, identify needs, healthier response and support; 3–5 minutes. Strong addiction/withdrawal safety boundary; never advise unsupervised detox or enduring severe symptoms.
- Compassion/accountability: emotion, facts vs assumptions, information then available, respected colleague perspective, appropriate responsibility and constructive next action; 2–4 minutes. No absolution, compulsory confession/trauma recount, legal advice or replacement of duty/reporting requirements.
